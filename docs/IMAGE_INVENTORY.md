## Selection and related compositions

- Hero: 749879147 provides the strongest summer exterior, showing the timber balconies, hanging chair and banana leaves. 519536076 shows a related wider angle; 484902845 is a useful portrait entrance view.
- Banana trees are clearly visible in 751549883 and also in the edges of exterior/terrace photographs. No foliage was added to any photograph.
- The two pool views complement each other: 749286981 shows the above-ground pool from above; 749286956 gives garden and trampoline context.
- 484903250 and 518965703 are similar terrace angles with different parasol arrangements. 751485665 is a distinct elevated view.
- Mehana photographs show complementary dining-room angles. The three bathrooms show fixtures and shower details, with no verified floor assignment.
- Bedroom images may depict related views of the same room. None is counted as proof of a separate bedroom or assigned to a floor.
- No identical SHA-256 hashes. All images are suitable for the complete gallery; overlapping compositions remain accurately captioned.
